Hospital Admission Policy

Patient Registration:
Patients must provide their full name, age, contact number, and valid identification proof during registration.

Admission Requirements:
Patients requiring hospitalization must receive an admission recommendation from a qualified doctor. Patients must provide their medical history and insurance details, if applicable.

Cancellation Policy:
Patients can cancel their appointments up to 24 hours before the scheduled appointment. Cancellations made less than 24 hours before the appointment may be subject to cancellation charges.

Discharge Procedure:
Before discharge, patients must settle outstanding bills and collect their discharge summary, prescriptions, and medical reports from the hospital.
